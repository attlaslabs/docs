The **Triggers** tab is where you add the questions and actions that will appear in the center of your chat.

Triggers act as conversation starters. They spark visitors curiosity and encourage them to interact with your chat within the first few seconds.

<Check>
  To add triggers, simply click on the **"Triggers"** tab in the left-side menu.
</Check>

![Attlas Triggers](/images/triggers_1.webp)

## Trigger types

Attlas offers **18 trigger types**, organized by category:

### Start with

- **Questions**: Prompt visitors with curated questions to start a conversation.
- **External links**: Direct users to external websites, landing pages, or stores.
- **Cards**: Highlight posts, products, or offers with a 16:9 visual cover image.
- **Files**: Offer downloadable PDFs, e-books, guides, or catalogs.

### Organize

- **Section**: Group and categorize your triggers under visual header dividers.
- **Space**: Add vertical spacing between trigger blocks for cleaner layouts.

### Media

- **Google Drive files**: Share documents, presentations, or spreadsheets from Google Drive.
- **Mini-video**: Upload a short video that visitors can play directly in the chat.
- **YouTube videos**: Showcase specific videos or tutorials inline.
- **YouTube channel**: Link to your channel to encourage subscriptions and video discovery.
- **Pinterest**: Display your Pinterest profile and its latest Pins directly in the chat.
- **Spotify**: Embed playable songs or playlists directly inside the chat.
- **SoundCloud**: Embed audio tracks or playlists with custom player height.

### Contact & location

- **Location maps**: Share interactive location maps and directions.
- **Email**: Provide an instant email contact action.
- **Phone**: Allow visitors to call your phone number directly.
- **WhatsApp**: Capture lead details (name, phone, message) before seamlessly redirecting to WhatsApp.

### Specialized

- **Next Dates (Bandsintown)**: Display live tour dates and show tickets for artists and performers.

### Questions

Add questions that encourage interaction and help users discover more about you, your organization, or your content.

**Examples:**

- Who are we?
- What products do we offer?
- How does this service work?
- Where can I find specific information?

Questions are one of the most effective ways to start conversations and guide users toward the topics that matter most.

![Question Trigger](/images/trigger-editor-question.webp)

<Note>
  **Title** is what users see before clicking. Make it short, clear and engaging. The **Question** remains hidden and will only be sent to the chat after the trigger is clicked.
</Note>

#### Generate questions

This action allows you to generate questions from a document that has already been added to the chat's knowledge.

![Generate Triggers](/images/generate-questions_1.webp)

Simply click the **AI Action** <kbd>Generate Questions</kbd>** button and Attlas will open a window where you can choose:

1. Select the document you want to generate questions from
2. Set the number of questions you want to generate

Within a few seconds, Attlas will automatically generate suggestions based on the content of the document.

![Generate Triggers](/images/generate-questions_2.webp)

You can then:

- Select the questions that make the most sense
- Remove the ones you don't want to use
- Choose a parent trigger from the **Target Parent** selector to group them under an existing foldable trigger or section (or leave it as **Root** to keep them top-level)
- Generate new suggestions if the initial results do not meet your expectations

When you're finished, click `Add` and you're done. The questions will be added immediately and can be tested in the **Playground**.

### Cards

Create visual link cards to capture your users' attention with a large cover image. 

Cards are perfect for highlighting:

- Blog posts and articles

The card includes a 16:9 cover image and a title. When you add a URL, the card becomes clickable, redirecting users to your page. Visitors can also easily copy the link directly from the card.

### SoundCloud embed

Embed audio tracks or playlists directly within the chat interface.

Just add a link to any public SoundCloud track or playlist. You can also adjust the player embed height in pixels (between 100px and 1000px, defaulting to 200px) to best fit your layout.

### Mini-video

Upload a short MP4 video to create a more personal and visual entry point in your chat. Visitors can play or pause it, turn the sound on or off, and see the remaining duration.

You can choose a **circle** or **rounded square** shape. You can also enable **Autoplay**. Autoplay videos always start muted, so visitors stay in control of the sound.

![Mini-video Trigger](/images/trigger-mini-video.webp)

<Warning>
  Mini-videos are uploaded from your device only. They must be MP4 files, no larger than 50 MB and no longer than 2 minutes. Direct video links and other video formats are not supported.
</Warning>

### WhatsApp

![WhatsApp Trigger](/images/trigger-whatsapp.webp)

Allow visitors to contact you on WhatsApp directly from your chat interface.

## Customizing Icons

By default, each trigger type comes with a preconfigured icon designed to represent its content (for example, a question mark for questions, a Spotify logo for Spotify music, or a paperclip for files).

For most trigger types, you can customize this icon to match your brand or preference.

### Available Customization Options

When editing an eligible trigger, click on the icon preview in the edit drawer to open the media picker. You can choose from:
- **Upload**: Upload a custom image file (PNG, JPG, SVG, etc.) from your device.
- **Link**: Provide a direct URL to an image hosted online.
- **Emoji**: Pick a standard emoji to display as the icon.
- **Icon**: Choose from a library of pre-selected system icons.
- **Unsplash**: Search and select high-quality free stock photos.

### Icon Support by Trigger Type

Not all trigger types support icon customization. Some have fixed layouts or default icons:

| Trigger Type | Customizable Icon | Default Icon |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Question** | Yes | Question Mark |
| **External link** | Yes | Link / Chain |
| **Card** | No (displays its own cover image) | None |
| **Spotify** | No | Spotify Logo |
| **SoundCloud** | No | SoundCloud Logo |
| **Mini-video** | No (displays its own video player) | None |
| **YouTube video** | No | YouTube Logo |
| **YouTube channel** | Yes | YouTube Logo |
| **Pinterest** | No | Pinterest Logo |

## Nested Triggers (Parent-Child)

To keep your chat interface clean and organized, you can nest triggers under other triggers to create a parent-child relationship.

### Foldable triggers

Only certain trigger types can act as a parent (foldable) and contain child triggers:

| Trigger Type | Foldable |
| --- | --- |
| Question | No |
| Link | Yes |
| Card | Yes |
| File | Yes |
| Google Drive | Yes |
| Mini-video | No |
| YouTube video | No |
| YouTube channel | No |
| Pinterest | No |
| Spotify | No |
| SoundCloud | No |
| Map | No |
| Concerts | No |
| WhatsApp | No |
| Email | No |
| Phone | No |
| Section | Yes |
| Space | No |

### How it works

When a trigger contains child triggers:
- **In the Chat:** Clicking the parent trigger expands it inline to reveal its children.
  - If the parent is a content trigger (such as a Link, File, or Card), the expansion displays a primary action row at the top (to visit the link or download the file), followed by the child questions.
  - If the parent is a **Section**, it expands to display the child items.

### How to set up nested triggers

<Frame caption="Drag trigger in group">
  <img src="/images/trigger-drag-in.webp" alt="Drag trigger in group" />
</Frame>

<Frame caption="Drag trigger out of group">
  <img src="/images/trigger-drag-out.webp" alt="Drag trigger out of group" />
</Frame>

You can create parent-child relationships in two ways:

1. **Drag and Drop:** In the trigger list, drag a trigger to the right (indenting it) to nest it under the trigger above it. Drag it back to the left (outdenting) to restore it as a top-level trigger.
2. **From the Editor:** Open the edit drawer of any parent-eligible trigger, scroll down to the **Suggested Questions** section and click <kbd>Add Question</kbd> to add questions directly as children.
