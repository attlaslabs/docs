The **Triggers** tab is where you add interactive link buttons, media embeds, and contact shortcuts that appear in the center of your chat.

Triggers help visitors discover your content, explore your resources, and take action without leaving the conversation.

<Check>
  To add triggers, click on the **"Triggers"** tab in the left-side menu.
</Check>

![Attlas Triggers](/images/triggers_1.webp)

## Trigger types

Attlas offers a wide variety of link and media trigger types organized by category:

### Start with

- **External links**: Direct users to external websites, landing pages, or resources.
- **Cards**: Highlight articles, announcements, or featured items with a visual 16:9 cover image.
- **Files**: Offer downloadable PDFs, e-books, guides, or catalogs.

### Organize

- **Section**: Group and categorize your triggers under clean header dividers.
- **Space**: Add vertical spacing between trigger blocks for a balanced layout.

### Media

- **Mini-video**: Upload a short video that visitors can play directly inside the chat.
- **YouTube videos**: Showcase specific videos or tutorials inline.
- **YouTube channel**: Link to your channel to encourage subscriptions and video discovery.
- **Pinterest**: Display your Pinterest profile and latest Pins directly in the chat.
- **Spotify**: Embed playable songs, albums, or playlists directly inside the chat.
- **SoundCloud**: Embed audio tracks or playlists with custom player height.
- **Google Drive files**: Share documents, presentations, or spreadsheets hosted on Google Drive.

### Contact & location

- **Location maps**: Share interactive location maps and directions.
- **WhatsApp**: Capture lead details (name, phone, message) before redirecting to WhatsApp.
- **Email**: Provide an instant email contact action.
- **Phone**: Allow visitors to call your phone number directly.

### Specialized

- **Next Dates (Bandsintown)**: Display live tour dates and ticket links for artists and performers.

## Content and links

### External links

Add links to important pages you want to share with your visitors.

You can share:

- Company websites
- Landing pages
- Blogs and articles
- Portfolios
- Registration or booking pages

![Trigger Link](/images/trigger-editor-link.webp)

---

### Cards

Create visual link cards to capture your visitors' attention with a large cover image.

Cards are ideal for highlighting:

- Blog posts and guides
- Special offers or announcements
- Important news
- Featured services

The card includes a 16:9 cover image and a title. When you add a URL, the card becomes clickable and redirects visitors to your page. Visitors can also copy the link directly from the card.

<Info>
  Attlas automatically adds UTM tracking parameters to help you analyze traffic sources.
</Info>

![Card Trigger](/images/trigger-editor-card.webp)

---

### Files

Make documents available for direct download through triggers.

Ideal for sharing:

- PDFs
- E-books
- Catalogs
- Guides and whitepapers
- Presentation slides

Visitors can download the document immediately without leaving the chat interface.

![File Trigger](/images/trigger-editor-file.webp)

### Google Drive files

Share documents, presentations, spreadsheets, or any other files stored in Google Drive.

Simply add the public share link to give visitors instant access to your content.

![Google Drive Trigger](/images/trigger-editor-google-drive.webp)

## Media embeds

### Mini-video

Upload a short MP4 video to create a personal and engaging entry point in your chat. Visitors can play or pause the video, toggle sound, and view remaining duration.

You can choose between a **circle** or **rounded square** shape, and enable **Autoplay**. Autoplay videos always start muted so visitors stay in control of the audio.

![Mini-video Trigger](/images/trigger-mini-video.webp)

<Warning>
  Mini-videos must be MP4 files uploaded from your device, no larger than 50 MB and no longer than 2 minutes.
</Warning>

---

### YouTube videos

Promote specific videos from your channel or third-party tutorials directly inside your chat.

Ideal for:

- Product walkthroughs
- Video tutorials
- Presentations
- Case studies

![YouTube Video Trigger](/images/trigger-editor-youtube-video.webp)

---

### YouTube channel

Add your channel link so visitors can discover your videos and subscribe easily.

![YouTube Channel Trigger](/images/trigger-editor-youtube-channel.webp)

---

### Spotify embed

Share music, podcasts, or playlists directly inside the chat interface.

Whether you are an artist, a podcaster, or curating recommendations for your audience, add any Spotify link to embed a playable widget.

![Spotify Trigger](/images/trigger-spotify.webp)

---

### SoundCloud embed

Embed audio tracks or playlists directly within the chat interface.

Add any public SoundCloud track or playlist link. You can also customize the embed height (between 100px and 1000px, defaulting to 200px) to suit your layout.

![SoundCloud Trigger](/images/trigger-soundcloud.webp)

---

### Pinterest

Share your Pinterest profile directly in your chat. Visitors can browse your latest Pins without leaving the conversation.

Enter your Pinterest username when configuring the trigger. Use only letters, numbers, underscores, and hyphens, without spaces or full URLs.

![Pinterest Trigger](/images/trigger-pinterest.webp)

## Contact and location

### Location maps

Share a location directly inside your chat to help visitors find your physical premises.

Add an address to display an interactive map pin with direct navigation links.

![Map Trigger](/images/trigger-editor-map.webp)

---

### WhatsApp

Allow visitors to contact you on WhatsApp directly from your chat.

Clicking a WhatsApp trigger opens an interactive lead capture drawer. Visitors can enter their name, phone number, and message. Attlas automatically saves the lead in your CRM before redirecting to WhatsApp with the message pre-filled.

![WhatsApp Trigger](/images/trigger-whatsapp.webp)

---

### Email and Phone

Provide one-tap communication triggers for visitors who prefer traditional contact methods.

- **Email**: Opens the visitor's default email client with your address pre-filled.
- **Phone**: Prompts the visitor's device to call your phone number directly.

---

### Next Dates (Bandsintown)

Display live tour dates and upcoming concert events directly in your chat through your connected Bandsintown profile.

![Bandsintown Trigger](/images/trigger-bandsintown.webp)

Visitors can browse upcoming tour dates, venues, locations, and access ticket links inside the conversation.

## Customizing icons

Each trigger type comes with a default icon. For most trigger types, you can customize this icon to match your brand.

### Customization options

Click on the icon preview in the edit drawer to open the media picker:

- **Upload**: Upload a custom image file (PNG, JPG, SVG) from your device.
- **Link**: Provide a direct URL to an image hosted online.
- **Emoji**: Pick a standard emoji.
- **Icon**: Choose from a library of system icons.
- **Unsplash**: Search and select free stock photos.

### Icon support by trigger type

| Trigger Type | Customizable Icon | Default Icon |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **External link** | Yes | Link / Chain |
| **Card** | No (displays its own cover image) | None |
| **File** | Yes | File / Paperclip |
| **Google Drive** | Yes | Google Drive Logo |

| **YouTube video** | No | YouTube Logo |

## Nested triggers (Parent-Child)

You can nest triggers under parent triggers to keep your chat interface compact and organized.

### Foldable triggers

Only certain trigger types can act as a parent (foldable) and contain child triggers:

| Trigger Type | Foldable |
| :--- | :--- |
| Link | Yes |
| Card | Yes |
| File | Yes |
| Google Drive | Yes |
| Section | Yes |
| Mini-video | No |
| YouTube video | No |
| YouTube channel | No |
| Pinterest | No |
| Spotify | No |
| SoundCloud | No |
| Map | No |
| Concerts | No |
| WhatsApp | No |
| Email | No |
| Phone | No |
| Space | No |

### How to set up nested triggers

<Frame caption="Drag trigger in group">
  <img src="/images/trigger-drag-in.webp" alt="Drag trigger in group" />
</Frame>

<Frame caption="Drag trigger out of group">
  <img src="/images/trigger-drag-out.webp" alt="Drag trigger out of group" />
</Frame>

You can create parent-child relationships in two ways:

1. **Drag and Drop**: In the trigger list, drag a trigger to the right (indenting it) to nest it under the trigger above it. Drag it back to the left (outdenting) to restore it as a top-level trigger.
2. **From the Editor**: Open the edit drawer of any parent-eligible trigger, scroll down to the **Suggested Questions** section, and click <kbd>Add Question</kbd> to add questions directly as children.
