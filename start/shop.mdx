The **Shop** tab allows you to showcase merchandise, physical items, digital goods, and services directly inside your chat.

When you add products, Attlas presents them in a dedicated 2-column store layout that makes browsing and purchasing simple for your visitors.

<Check>
  To manage your catalog, navigate to the **Triggers** page in your dashboard and select the **Shop** tab.
</Check>

![Product Trigger](/images/trigger-product.webp)

## Setting up a product

Each product trigger includes:

- **Cover image**: High-resolution visual showcasing your item. Required to display the card.
- **Title**: Clear name for the product.
- **Price and currency**: Item price and 3-letter currency code (e.g. USD, EUR, GBP, BRL, CAD).
- **URL**: Destination link pointing directly to your checkout page, online store, or payment link (such as Stripe, Shopify, or Gumroad).

<Info>
  **Automatic currency display**: Attlas automatically converts and displays the equivalent price in your visitor's local currency based on their language and region.
</Info>

## Organizing with categories

You can organize products into categories using **Section** triggers (such as "Apparel", "Prints", "Courses", or "Accessories").

To create a category:

1. In the **Shop** tab, click **Product** or the **+** button.
2. Select **Section** and name your category.
3. Drag product cards under the section to nest them.

<Tip>
  Section headers in the Shop tab span across both columns of the product grid to introduce each group clearly.
</Tip>

<Note>
  The Shop tab only accepts **Product** cards and **Section** category headers. All other trigger types (links, files, contact buttons, maps) belong in the **Links** tab.
</Note>

## Choosing your tab label: Shop or Catalogue

You can customize the display name of your store tab to match your business:

1. Open the **Triggers** page.
2. Click the small arrow next to the **Shop** tab header.
3. Choose between **Shop** and **Catalogue**.

| Label | When to use |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Shop** | Your product links lead directly to a checkout page or shopping cart where visitors purchase online immediately. |
| **Catalogue** | Your product links lead to portfolio items, real estate listings, or quote request pages where visitors browse before getting in touch. |

---

## Setting the default tab

When you have at least one product in your Shop tab, Attlas reveals a **Shown first** dropdown menu in the tab header.

You can select which tab visitors see first when opening your chat:

- **Links**: Shows your general links, cards, and media embeds first.
- **Shop** (or **Catalogue**): Shows your product grid first. Ideal when selling or showcasing inventory is the main goal of your chat.

Visitors can still switch between tabs at any time using the switcher at the top of the chat.

## Best practices

- **Consistent cover images**: Use photos with matching aspect ratios to give your product grid a clean look.
- **Direct checkout links**: Send visitors straight to the checkout or item purchase page to minimize friction.
- **Concise titles**: Keep product names short so they fit neatly on mobile screens.
