The **Shop** tab allows you to showcase merchandise, physical items, digital products, and services directly inside your chat.

When you add products, Attlas presents them in a dedicated 2-column store layout that makes browsing and purchasing seamless for your visitors.

<Check>
  To manage your catalog, navigate to the **"Triggers"** page and switch to the **"Shop"** tab.
</Check>

![Product Trigger](/images/trigger-product.webp)

## Setting up a product

Each product trigger includes:

- **Cover image**: High-quality visual showcasing your item.
- **Product title**: Clear and concise name for the item.
- **Price and currency**: Amount and currency code (supporting USD, EUR, GBP, BRL, CAD, JPY, and more).
- **Destination URL**: Outbound link pointing directly to your checkout page, online store, or payment processor (e.g. Stripe, Shopify, Gumroad).

<Info>
  **Automatic currency display**: When visitors browse your chat in their preferred language, Attlas automatically converts and displays the equivalent price in their local currency.
</Info>

## Links and Shop tabs

<video autoPlay muted loop playsInline src="/videos/triggers-tabs.mp4" />

In your chat settings, your triggers are organized across tabs:

1. **Links & Media**: For your links, cards, embeds, and contact shortcuts.
2. **Shop**: For your products and store collections.
3. **Questions**: For suggested conversation starters.

When you have at least one product in your Shop, Attlas automatically adds a **Links / Shop switcher** at the top of your public chat. Visitors can easily toggle between your general links and your store items.

---

## Organizing with sections

You can add **Section** dividers in your Shop tab to categorize your products (e.g. "Apparel", "E-books", "Accessories", "Courses").

<Tip>
  Section headers in the Shop tab automatically span across both columns of the product grid to keep your catalog clean and easy to browse.
</Tip>

---

## Best practices

- **High-resolution images**: Use clean, well-lit photos with consistent aspect ratios for a professional storefront look.
- **Direct checkout links**: Link directly to the specific product checkout or cart URL to minimize friction for the buyer.
- **Descriptive titles**: Keep product titles short so they fit neatly on mobile screens.
