Once your chat has been configured and trained, it's time to share it with the world.

Attlas provides multiple distribution methods so you can choose the one that best fits your audience, channel, or project.

To access these options, click the **<kbd>Share</kbd>** button located in the top-right corner of the platform.

![Sharing Options 1](/images/sharing-options.webp)

---

## Sharing options

Here are the 3 main ways to share your chat:

1. **Direct link**: Perfect for social media, emails and messaging apps.
2. **Scan & Chat (QR Code)**: Ideal for printed materials and physical locations.
3. **Embed on your website**: Three modes, **Standard** (inline), **Bubble** (floating button) and **Popup** (opens from your own button).

---

## Direct link

The direct link lets you share a full-page version of your chat. You can use it across various platforms:

- **Social media**: Share links directly in posts or bio links (Instagram bio, LinkedIn, Linktree).
- **Messaging**: Send directly via WhatsApp, SMS, or Telegram.
- **Email**: Add to newsletters, campaigns, or email signatures.
- **Promotional materials**: Add to presentations, PDFs, or documents.

Simply copy the generated public link from your dashboard and share it with your audience.

---

## Scan & Chat (QR Code)

Connect the physical world to your AI chat by generating a custom QR Code. It is highly effective for:

- **Printed materials**: Business cards, flyers, catalogs and booklets.
- **Physical locations**: Restaurant menus, tables, packaging, or posters.
- **Events**: Banners, trade show stands and presentations.

When scanned, users will be taken directly to your chat in their mobile browser.

<Info>
  If you change your username in the **Profile** section, you will need to generate and download a new QR Code.
</Info>

## Embed on your website

The **Embed on your website** option puts your chat on your own pages. Pick one of three modes, preview it live, then copy the code.

| Mode | What visitors see | Plan |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Standard** | The chat sits inside your page, like an image or a video. | All plans |
| **Bubble** | A floating button in the corner of every page. It opens the chat in a small panel. | Starter and PRO |
| **Popup** | The chat opens in a window over your page when someone clicks your own button or link. | Starter and PRO |

Switch between modes at the top of the panel. Click **Get the code** to see the snippet, or **Back to preview** to return.

<Note>
  The bubble and the popup are available on the **Attlas Starter and PRO plans**. On the Free plan, the panel shows an **Upgrade** button instead of the code.
</Note>

### Options

| Option | Applies to | What it does |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Chat profile** | All modes | Show or hide the avatar, name, description, social links and share button. |
| **Height** | Standard | Height of the chat on your page, in pixels (minimum 300). |
| **Button text** | Popup | The label of the button we generate for you. |
| **Width** | Popup | Width of the popup window, in pixels (minimum 320, default 440). |

### Standard

Paste the code where the chat should appear: a help center, a documentation site, a support portal or a Notion page.

![Attlas Standard Chat Site](/images/sharing-standard.webp)

```html
<iframe
  src="https://attlas.so/your-chat-slug?embed=inline"
  width="100%"
  height="700"
  style="border:none;">
</iframe>
```

<Tip>
  The chat fills the frame with no card around it. Set the width and height to fit your layout.
</Tip>

### Bubble

Paste this tag at the end of every page that should show the bubble:

```html
<script src="https://attlas.so/embed.js" id="YOUR_CHAT_ID" async></script>
```

The color, position and icon of the bubble come from your chat settings. Use the **Customize the bubble** link in the panel to change them.

![Attlas Bubble Chat](/images/sharing-bubble.webp)

### Popup

The popup has no button of its own. You choose what opens it, so it fits your design.

**1. Add the script** once, at the end of your page:

```html
<script src="https://attlas.so/embed.js" id="YOUR_CHAT_ID" data-mode="popup" async></script>
```

![Attlas Popup Chat](/images/sharing-popup.webp)

**2. Add a button.** Any element with the `data-attlas-open` attribute opens the chat: a button, a div or an image.

```html
<button data-attlas-open="YOUR_CHAT_ID">Chat with us</button>
```

**Site builders without custom attributes.** Point any link to this address instead:

```html
<a href="#attlas-open=YOUR_CHAT_ID">Chat with us</a>
```

**Your own code.** Open and close the popup from JavaScript:

```js
Attlas.open("YOUR_CHAT_ID", { width: 440 });
Attlas.close("YOUR_CHAT_ID");
```

The panel fills in your chat ID, button text and width for you.

### Domain whitelisting

To protect your quota and make sure the chat only loads on your own website, restrict the domains allowed to run the script.

![Allowed domains](/images/sharing-domains.webp)

- **How it works:** In the **Allowed domains** section under the Bubble and Popup code, add the domains you use (for example `mywebsite.com`). The script only starts on these domains.
- **No domain listed (default):** The script works on **any domain**, which is handy for local testing.

<Warning>
  The whitelist applies to the **Bubble** and **Popup** scripts only. It does not restrict the **Standard** iframe. Anyone who embeds the iframe URL on their own page can still display and use the chat.
</Warning>

## Distribution channels

The **Distribution** tab in the Share panel lets you create named channels to track where your conversations are coming from, for example, distinguishing users who arrived via your website from those who came through a newsletter or a QR code at an event.

### What is a channel?

A channel is a label you define (e.g. `website`, `newsletter`, `event-june`). When you select it in the Share tab, Attlas automatically appends an `?origin=<channel>` parameter to every share method you copy:

| Share method | With channel selected |
| :-- | :-- |
| Direct link | `https://attlas.so/your-chat?origin=website` |
| Standard (iframe) | `src="https://attlas.so/your-chat?embed=inline&origin=website"` |
| Bubble and Popup script | `<script ... data-origin="website" ...>` |

This lets you see in your analytics which channel drove each conversation.

### Creating a channel

 ![Distribution channels tab](/images/sharing-channel_1.webp)


1. Open the **Share** panel and switch to the **Distribution** tab.
2. Click in the channel input field and type a name (e.g. `newsletter`), then press **Enter** to add it.
3. Repeat to add as many channels as you need.
4. Channels are saved automatically, no extra confirmation needed.

<Info>
  Channel names are slugified automatically: spaces and special characters are converted to hyphens and lowercased (e.g. `My Blog Post` → `my-blog-post`).
</Info>

### Using a channel when sharing

 ![Distribution channels link sharing](/images/sharing-channel_2.webp)


1. Switch back to the **Share** tab.
2. If you have channels defined, a dropdown appears at the top of the tab.
3. Select the channel that matches the destination you are sharing to.
4. Copy the link or the embed code, the `origin` parameter is already included.

<Tip>
  Leave the channel selector on **All channels** when you want a generic link without attribution.
</Tip>

## Which option should you choose?

Each sharing method serves a different purpose:

| Option | Best for | Channel tracking | Domain Whitelisting |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Direct link** | Quick sharing across any digital channel | Yes (`?origin=`) | No |
| **Scan & Chat** | Physical materials and QR Codes | Yes (encoded in QR) | No |
| **Embed: Standard** | The full chat inline in a page | Yes (`origin=`) | No (bypasses domain checks) |
| **Embed: Bubble** | A floating assistant on every page | Yes (`data-origin=`) | **Yes** |
| **Embed: Popup** | A chat that opens from your own button or link | Yes (`data-origin=`) | **Yes** |

You can use one or several of these options at the same time, creating more opportunities for users to interact with your chat.
