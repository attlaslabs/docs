AI Reports analyze your chat's historical interactions, visitor behavior, and knowledge coverage to deliver actionable insights and recommendations.

<Note>
  AI Reports is a **Pro feature**. Upgrade your workspace plan to generate and export custom AI reports.
</Note>

![Attlas Reports](/images/reports.webp)

<Check>
  To access AI Reports, click the **Reports** button in the top-right corner of the **Overview** dashboard, or navigate directly to `/overview/your-chat-slug/reports`.
</Check>

---

## Generating a Report

1. Click **Reports** in the top navigation of the Overview dashboard.
2. Click the **Generate Report** button.
3. Select your desired time period:
   - **Pre-set ranges:** Last 7 days, 14 days, 30 days, 90 days, or 12 months.
   - **Custom range:** Select precise start and end dates using the calendar picker.
4. Choose your preferred **Report Language** (for example English, French, Portuguese, Spanish, German, or Japanese). The report will be generated entirely in the selected language.
5. Click **Generate**. Attlas will analyze your chat history and generate your report in seconds.

<Info>
  Available historical date ranges depend on your workspace plan level.
</Info>

---

## What's Included in a Report

Each AI Report compiles data and AI analysis into actionable sections:

- **Executive Summary:** Key highlights, top performing areas, and primary takeaways.
- **Audience & Traffic Channels:** Detailed breakdown of top countries, cities, device types, and visitor acquisition sources.
- **Engagement & Activity:** Visual charts of conversation volume, message counts, funnel conversion, and top clicked triggers.
- **What Worked vs What Didn't:** Clear breakdown of successful user interactions compared to areas where visitors experienced friction.
- **Blind Spots & Knowledge Gaps:** Highlights questions where the AI provided weak or incomplete responses, showing you exactly what information is missing from your knowledge base.
- **Actionable Recommendations:** Tailored, step-by-step suggestions generated by AI to help you optimize triggers, update training documents, and increase response quality.

## Downloading and Exporting

You can download any generated report for offline review, sharing with team members, or archiving by clicking the **Download** button at the top of the report view.
