### Short description

Attlas turns your knowledge and online presence into an interactive conversational AI chat. Add your links, documents, videos, and notes once: Attlas answers visitor questions with zero hallucinations, books calendar appointments, captures qualified leads, and brings you into the loop when needed.

### Boilerplate description

Attlas is a conversational platform that transforms how creators, businesses, and teams share knowledge and interact with their audience. By turning scattered content (websites, documents, media, and workspaces) into an intelligent conversational assistant, Attlas enables visitors to get instant, accurate answers in over 80 languages, book appointments in real time, and take action immediately. Built with radical simplicity, Attlas delivers a shareable, embeddable conversational experience with zero technical overhead and complete control over what is said.

## Brand rules

To maintain brand consistency across all mediums, follow these principles:

- **Provide clearspace**: Maintain generous padding around all marks so they remain distinct and legible.
- **Maintain contrast**: Use the light marks on light backgrounds and the dark marks on dark backgrounds.
- **Do not alter or stretch**: Never distort aspect ratios, change individual letterforms, or apply harsh drop shadows.
