The **CRM & Contacts** section serves as a built-in CRM for managing all leads collected through your Attlas chatbots. 

Whenever a visitor interacts with a lead capture trigger, fills out contact details, or books an appointment during a conversation, their information is automatically saved and organized into your workspace contacts.

---

## Accessing your contacts

<Check>
  To view your leads, click on the **<kbd>CRM</kbd>** tab located in the left-hand sidebar of your workspace.
</Check>

From here, you can switch between workspace environments using the workspace selector at the top of the page.

---

## Views: Table and Pipeline (Kanban)

Attlas provides two complementary views to help you organize and process leads according to your workflow.

### Table View

![CRM table view](/images/crm-list.webp)

The **Table View** displays all collected leads in a structured list. It is ideal for searching, filtering, and performing batch operations on large numbers of contacts.

| Field | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Contact Info** | Name, email address, phone number, and WhatsApp details provided by the visitor. |
| **Source Chat** | The specific chat that captured the lead. |
| **Origin Channel** | The entry point or embedded channel where the interaction took place. |
| **Language** | The language used by the visitor during the conversation. |
| **Appointment** | Scheduled calendar booking date and status (if applicable). |
| **Stage** | Current status in your sales pipeline. |
| **Date** | Timestamp when the contact was first recorded. |

#### Filtering and Exporting
- **Filter by Chatbot**: Narrow down your contact list to leads collected by a specific chatbot.
- **Search**: Locate contacts instantly by name, email, or phone number.
- **Export to CSV**: Click the **<kbd>Export CSV</kbd>** button to download your lead list for use in external tools like HubSpot, Salesforce, or Mailchimp.
- **Bulk Delete**: Select multiple rows to delete unwanted test entries or old contacts in bulk.

---

### Board (Kanban) View

![CRM Kanban view](/images/crm-kanban.webp)

The **Board View** organizes your leads into visual columns representing your sales pipeline. This view gives you a clear overview of deal progress and helps prioritize follow-up activities.

Leads move through 5 customizable status stages:

| Stage | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **New** | Newly captured leads that require initial review or outreach. |
| **Contacted** | Leads you or your team have reached out to. |
| **Qualified** | Leads that meet your criteria and show clear interest. |
| **Won** | Successfully converted leads or closed deals. |
| **Lost** | Leads that did not convert or were archived. |

<Tip>
  You can drag and drop lead cards between stage columns to update their pipeline status instantly.
</Tip>

## Contact details and conversation history

![CRM contact details](/images/crm-contact.webp)

Clicking on any contact in either Table or Board view opens the detailed contact modal.

### Lead Details and Notes
- **Edit Pipeline Stage**: Change stage status directly from the dropdown menu.
- **Add Notes**: Keep context-rich notes for your team (for example, specific customer requirements or call summaries). Notes save automatically.
- **Appointments**: Review connected calendar bookings and meeting details.

### Inspecting Conversation History
Switch to the **Conversation** tab inside the contact modal to view the exact transcript where the lead was captured. Reviewing the conversation context helps you understand visitor intent before making outreach.

---

## Data privacy and encryption

Attlas protects your visitors' sensitive contact details by default.

<Note>
  Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as email addresses, phone numbers, and WhatsApp numbers are encrypted at rest on Attlas servers. They are decrypted securely on-the-fly only when authorized workspace members view the contacts interface.
</Note>
