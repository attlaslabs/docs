The agent is a chat inside your dashboard. You type a request in plain words, and it reads your chat's data or changes the chat for you. It relies on the same core tools as our Workspace MCP (spanning 50 read and write capabilities, while delete actions remain reserved for the dashboard).

For example, you can ask:

- "How many visitors did my chat get this week, and where did they come from?"
- "Which questions did my chat answer badly?"
- "Add three suggested questions about my opening hours."
- "Switch to a darker theme."

<Note>
  Each request you send to the agent uses one **agent message**. Your plan includes a monthly allowance:

  | Plan | Agent messages per month |
  |---|---|
  | Free | 20 |
  | Starter | 100 |
  | Pro | 300 |

  When you run out, the agent stops answering until your allowance renews. You can upgrade your plan to get more.
</Note>

## Open the agent

1. Open one of your chats in the dashboard.
2. Click **Agent** in the top bar. On mobile, tap the round **Agent** button at the bottom of the screen.

The agent works on the chat you have open. Your chat preview stays visible on the right, so you see each change as soon as it applies.

<Info>
  Only members of your workspace can use the agent. A member with the **Viewer** role can ask questions but can't change anything.
</Info>

## Assisted chat creation

When creating a new chat or completing your initial onboarding, the agent can build your chat with you through a guided screen:

1. Click **Create with assistant** in the new chat dialog, or start from the onboarding flow.
2. Provide your website address, an Instagram handle, or simply your business name.
3. The agent reads your public profile, extracts your logo or photo as an avatar, writes your greeting, and drafts your first questions.
4. You answer 5 or 6 short questions about your business to fill in details that cannot be found online.
5. When finished, you get a direct share link, a downloadable QR code, and an interactive preview to test your chat before publishing.

## Start from your top improvements

When you open a new conversation, the agent shows **Top improvements**: the three most useful things missing from your chat, such as knowledge, a greeting, or suggested questions. You also see your coverage rate and the number of leads from the last 30 days.

Click an improvement and the agent walks you through it, one question at a time.

## Choose how changes apply

The mode selector sits in the message box.

| Mode | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Manual** (default) | The agent shows each change on a card. You click **Apply** to make it, or **Refuse** to skip it. |
| **Auto** | The agent makes changes right away and shows a card for each one it applied. |

When the agent proposes several changes in a single turn, **Apply all** and **Refuse all** buttons appear so you can review everything in one click.

<Warning>
  The agent never deletes anything, in either mode. To delete a trigger, a document or a contact, use the matching screen in the dashboard.

  In **Auto** mode, text written by your visitors or found in your documents can steer a change you didn't ask for. Stay in **Manual** if you want to check every change first.
</Warning>

## What the agent can do

| Read | Change |
|---|---|
| Visits, leads and conversion figures | Suggested questions (triggers): add, edit, reorder, group |
| Visitor conversations | Theme, branding, avatar and social links |
| Contacts and appointments | Goal and personality |
| Questions your chat answered badly | Business info, description, greeting and SEO |
| Knowledge documents | Knowledge: add a link or a file, write a document, add a correction, clean a document |
| Reports | Distribution channels: create named channels (website, Instagram bio, newsletter) to tag incoming conversations |
| Web page previews (product price, image and description) | Generate a new report |
| Instagram and LinkedIn profiles | |
| Embed code for your website | |
| Product skills and playbooks | |

The agent can also advise you: which feature fits your goal, how to organize your knowledge, which questions your visitors expect.

Some settings stay in the dashboard. The agent points you to the right screen when you ask for:

- deleting anything,
- changing your chat's URL,
- connecting an integration (Google Calendar, Notion, a database, Bandsintown, the Meta Conversions API, a custom domain, a webhook),
- access mode, password or visibility,
- your plan, billing and workspace members.

## Attach files and images

Click the attachment button in the message box to add up to 5 files. The agent reads PDFs and images (PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF) to answer you. Ask it to add a file to your knowledge and it imports it the same way as the [Sources](/start/sources) window. Audio files count against your quota the same way too.

---

## Web search

The agent can search the web, for example to check your opening hours on a public listing. It shows the sources it used under its answer.

Once the agent has searched the web in a conversation, every change waits for you to click **Apply**, even in **Auto** mode.

<Tip>
  You can also drive your workspace from Claude, Cursor or another AI assistant, with the same actions and advice. See [Workspace MCP](/mcp/workspace).
</Tip>
